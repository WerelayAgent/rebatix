Rebatix is a token launchpad on Solana where every buy comes with bonus tokens on top, and holders share payouts from every trade, paid directly in the token's pair token (USDG, ETH, etc.). Rebranded from Rebate Pad to **Rebatix** ($RBTX).

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Bonus Tokens on Every Buy**: Every buyer starts at least 1% to 9% ahead at the pre-trade price. Bonus tokens are added on top of the base swap output automatically.
- **Holder Payouts in Pair Token**: Every trade distributes payouts directly to holders in the paired asset (USDG, ETH, etc.). No staking, no claiming, no points.
- **Time-Weighted Hold Multiplier**: Shares scale by duration held (1× for Days 0–3, 1.5× for Days 3–7, 2× for Day 7+).
- **Automated Dip-Buying & Burn**: 60% of platform fees execute automated limit order dip-buying under the $RBTX price, and 40% execute market buybacks and burns.
- **Immutable Rules at Launch**: Rates for bonus tokens, fees, and holder distributions are locked permanently upon creation.
