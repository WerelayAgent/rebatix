02What Rebatix is

Rebatix is software. It gives you an interface to smart contracts on Solana that let anyone create a token with fixed rules and trade it through a DEX liquidity pool. Each launch sets its bonus rate (bonus tokens on buys, 2% to 9%), which is also its sell fee, its holder payout rate, and its payout destination. Those rates are written into the pool at launch and cannot be changed afterwards.

Rebatix is an independent project. It runs on Solana and uses DEX protocols, but it is not operated, endorsed or sponsored by Solana or Raydium.

04Non-custodial

We never hold your tokens, your funds or your keys. Every launch, trade and transfer is a transaction you sign in your own wallet and send to public smart contracts. We cannot reverse, cancel, pause or recover a transaction once it is sent, and we cannot move assets out of your wallet.

The rates of a pool are fixed when it is created and cannot be changed by anyone, including us. Some protocol-wide settings can be changed by an administrator: which pair tokens new launches may use, the launch fee (up to 0.1 ETH), and the limits of the automated $RBTX rounds (for example how often they can run and the cap on the caller’s share). Those settings never change the rates of an existing pool.

06Quotes, trades and fees

A quote shows the expected base tokens, bonus tokens, fees and total at the current price. It is an estimate. The final result can differ because of price movement, slippage, other transactions in the same block, and the following rules of the contracts:

  • Bonus tokens are paid from the token’s locked treasury and are capped at what the treasury holds at that moment.
  • When a launch has snipe protection on, buys in its first ten seconds pay a snipe fee in the pair token that starts at 99% and halves every second. It goes where the launch’s payouts go, to its holders or to its creator. Wallets the creator exempted at launch do not pay it.
  • Sells receive no bonus. They pay a sell fee, in tokens, equal to the launch’s bonus rate, which goes into the treasury.

Every trade on a launched token pays a 0.7% platform fee and the launch’s holder payout rate in the pair token, and a 0.3% pool fee, in the pair token on buys and in the token on sells. Trades of $RBTX pay a 0.2% platform fee instead. Together the fees on any token never exceed 10%. You also pay the network’s gas fees, and creating a token may carry a launch fee. Fees are taken by the smart contracts as each transaction runs and cannot be refunded.

You are responsible for any taxes that apply to your activity.

08Bonus tokens, holder payouts and $RBTX

Bonus tokens are extra tokens added to the base output of a buy. They are not a discount, a refund, tax relief, interest or protection against price changes.

Holder payouts are a share of trading fees in the pair token. They depend entirely on trading volume and can be small or zero. A holder’s share depends on their balance and how long they have held. Holdings worth under $1 are left out of a round, and a launch can exclude up to ten wallets, such as pools and contracts. Payouts are calculated and sent through the day by off-chain services run by us or by service providers, so they can be delayed or interrupted.

$RBTX is the Rebatix token. The platform fees are converted to USDG and used to buy $RBTX and burn it, through automated buy orders below its price and buys at the market price. Anyone may trigger a round once enough has built up, and the caller receives a small, capped share. Buying and burning reduces the supply of $RBTX. It does not guarantee any price or value.

They may carry eligibility or transfer restrictions, may be unavailable where you are, and can be paused, frozen or changed by their issuers. They give you no rights against Rebatix.
